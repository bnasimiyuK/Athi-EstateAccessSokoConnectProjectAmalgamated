About Athi Highway Estate

MARA-led governance
OUR VISION

A safe, connected and self-reliant community.

To be the model estate community in Kenya — a place where every household feels secure, every voice is heard, and residents work together to build a clean, green and thriving environment for generations to come.

OUR MISSION

To serve every resident with integrity, efficiency and care.

We will provide transparent governance, reliable shared services (security, waste, water, road maintenance), and platforms that connect residents to trusted local service providers.

What we are working towards

Security & Safety

24/7 controlled access and rapid response with local authorities.

Clean Environment

Reliable garbage collection, waste segregation, and clean common areas.

Water & Utilities

Continuous supply, fair billing, and maintained infrastructure.

Infrastructure

Roads, drainage and street lighting kept functional and safe.

Community Engagement

Regular MARA meetings and open decision making.

Sustainability

Tree planting, energy efficiency and responsible resource use.

Financial Health

Prudent budgeting, transparent accounting and timely audits.

Privacy & Data

We collect and process personal information only to the extent needed to run estate services. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

  1. What we collect Name, phone, email, house number, vehicle details and any information you submit.
  2. Why we collect it To verify residents, manage visitor pre-registration, deliver access codes, and provide estate services.
  3. Who can access it Authorised MARA officials and estate security personnel, limited by role.
  4. How long we keep it As required by our constitution and applicable law, then securely deleted.
  5. Sharing Only with trusted providers and public authorities where necessary. Never for advertising.
  6. Your rights Request access, correction or deletion by emailing MARA.
  7. Data security Password protection, encrypted connections, role-based access and periodic audits.
  8. Questions Contact beverly.kongani@gmail.com.
