Our Vision

A safe, connected and self-reliant community.

To be the model estate community in Kenya — a place where every household feels secure, every voice is heard, and residents work together to build a clean, green and thriving environment for generations to come.

Our Mission

To serve every resident with integrity, efficiency and care.

We will provide transparent governance, reliable shared services (security, waste, water, road maintenance), and platforms that connect residents to trusted local service providers — all while fostering a strong sense of community ownership.

Strategic Objectives

What we are working towards

Our committee measures progress against seven core objectives:

1. Security & Safety

Maintain 24/7 controlled access, trained guards and a rapid response protocol with local authorities.

2. Clean Environment

Reliable garbage collection, waste segregation, and a culture of “keep Athi clean”.

3. Water & Utilities

Ensure continuous supply, fair billing and maintain critical infrastructure in partnership with the county.

4. Infrastructure

Keep roads, drainage and street lighting functional and safe.

5. Community Engagement

Regular MARA meetings, transparent communication and open decision making.

6. Sustainability

Promote tree planting, energy efficiency and responsible resource use across the estate.

7. Financial Health

Prudent budgeting, transparent accounting and timely audits of all estate funds.

MARA

Management & Residents Association

The Mavoko Residents Association (MARA) is a community organization in Mavoko Sub-county, Machakos County, Kenya, that represents local residents' interests regarding land rights, urban development, and public service delivery

Key responsibilities

  • Collect and manage the monthly service charge
  • Appoint, monitor and pay estate service providers
  • Enforce the estate’s rules and by-laws fairly and consistently
  • Represent the estate in matters with the county and utilities
  • Organise the Annual General Meeting (AGM) and report on finances

Background

Athi Highway Estate (AHE)

Athi Highway Estate is a residential community located along the Nairobi–Mombasa highway in Athi River, Machakos County. The estate was developed in phases (Phase 1 and Phase 2) and is home to hundreds of families. Over the years, the community has grown organically, and with that growth came the need for structured self-governance.

MARA was established to formalise this self-governance and give every resident a seat at the table. Today, AHE and MARA work hand-in-hand to deliver reliable services, maintain common infrastructure, and keep the estate a place residents are proud to call home.

Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

We collect and process personal information only to the extent needed to run estate services. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. This summary explains the key points — the full policy is available on request.

  1. What we collect Name, phone, email, house number, vehicle details and any information you submit through resident, visitor or provider forms.
  2. Why we collect it To verify residents, manage visitor pre-registration, deliver access codes, coordinate security, and provide estate services.
  3. Who can access it Authorised MARA officials and estate security personnel. Access is logged and limited by role.
  4. How long we keep it Resident and visit records are kept for the period required by our constitution and any applicable law, then securely deleted.
  5. Sharing We share only with trusted service providers and public authorities where necessary (for example, in a security emergency). Never for advertising.
  6. Your rights You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing MARA. We respond within 30 days.
  7. Data security We use password protection, encrypted connections, role-based access and periodic audits to protect stored data.
  8. Questions Contact mara@athihighwayestate.or.ke for anything related to privacy.
