Safety first
  • Only board a trip with a matching 4-digit trip code.
  • Confirm the driver's estate house number matches the profile before entering the car.
  • Share the trip code with a family member or a friend outside the estate.
  • Report any discomfort immediately using the Report button on the trip.
  • Payment is arranged directly with the driver. The estate takes no commission.
Car pooling is a resident-to-resident service. The estate does not operate, insure, or profit from any trip.
